Taipei Art Book Fair · Since 2016

草率季

「如果可以草莽率性，
誰想要一本正經？」

從 2016 年開始，草率季每年聚集世界各地的創作者、出版社、獨立書店與藝術家，展出各種紙本刊物、攝影集、藝術書籍，也展各種創作作品、藝術計畫，以及這些作品背後獨一無二的靈魂。構成出版、學習、藝術、空間、體驗的大型共同創作。

Since 2016, Taipei Art Book Fair has brought together creators, publishers, independent bookstores, and artists from around the world — showcasing printed materials, photography, and art books alongside the unique spirits behind each creation. A large-scale collaborative platform that interweaves publishing, learning, art, space, and experience.

Zine
Wall

草率季 Zine Wall 是一面線上書牆，收錄歷年參展出版品的圖像與資訊。從獨立刊物、手冊到藝術書籍，每年的參展作品都留存於此，構成草率季的年度紙本地圖。

The Zine Wall is a wall of zine. It collects images and information of the publications exhibited in the Taipei Art Book Fair over the years. From independent publications and manuals to art books, the works exhibited each year are preserved here, forming an annual paper map of the Taipei Art Book Fair.

+
Fair
Archive

從 2016 至今，草率季的展覽現場、攤位記錄與活動照片都保存於 Fair-Archive。作為藝術書展的文獻見證，這裡是每一年草率季留給未來的座標。

Since 2016, the exhibition sites, booth records, and event photos of Taipei Art Book Fair have been preserved in the Fair-Archive. As a documentary witness of the art book fair, this is the coordinate that Taipei Art Book Fair leaves for the future.

Pretty
Fly
Books

Pretty Fly Books by TPABF 是草率季開設的實體書店，坐落於台北西門新宿 1F。常態販售藝術出版品，也跟著草率季走進各地書展現場。

Pretty Fly Books by TPABF is a physical bookstore run by Taipei Art Book Fair, located on the first floor of Ximen Shinshu in Taipei. It regularly sells art publications and participates in book fairs alongside Taipei Art Book Fair.

營業時間 12:00–19:00（二–日）

Polymer
Studio

Polymer 是草率季的藝術家工作室空間，提供創作、實驗與跨域媒合的場域，讓出版與藝術實踐在同一屋簷下生長、對話。

Polymer is the artist studio space of Taipei Art Book Fair, providing a venue for creation, experimentation, and cross-disciplinary collaboration, allowing publishing and artistic practice to grow and converse under the same roof.
