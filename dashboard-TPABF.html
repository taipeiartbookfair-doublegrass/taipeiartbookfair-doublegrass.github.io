草率季」是臺灣大型藝術書刊的文化市集，也是臺灣首度串連各種理念的創作者、出版社、獨立書店、藝術家等的市集，展出各式畫冊、獨立刊物、藝術雜誌等特色刊物。臺灣目前正在形成在地文化底蘊的背景下，這份狂野與不確定性的特質，吸引了亞洲如韓國、日本等藝術創作者前來共襄盛舉，草率季漸漸成為臺灣與國際間出版連結的大型藝術書刊市集。從 2016 第一屆草率季開始，每年都規劃不同主題、廣邀國內外各領域藝文創作者，提供各個參展單位表達自我與理想的交流平台。

FAQ

1 Q: 如何確認是否付款成功？
A: 完成付款後，系統會自動寄出付款確認信至您填寫的信箱。團隊將於指定時間統一更新「攤商管理」頁面中的核帳狀態。 		Q: How do I confirm if my payment was successful?
A: After completing payment, a confirmation email will be sent to your registered email. The team will update the Exhibitor Status on the "Dashboard" at the specified time.
2 Q: 沒有收到付款確認信怎麼辦？
A: 請先確認垃圾郵件夾；如仍未收到，請截圖您的付款頁面或紀錄，並來信與我們聯繫。 		Q: What if I didn't receive the payment confirmation email?
A: Please check your spam folder first. If you still haven't received it, take a screenshot of your payment page or record and contact us.
3 Q: 我已付款，但錄取狀態沒有更新？
A: 請耐心等待，團隊會於公告時間統一更新核帳狀態。如有急件或疑問，請附上付款截圖來信詢問。 		Q: I have paid, but my Exhibitor Status hasn't been updated?
A: Please be patient. The TPABF team will update the Exhibitor Status at the announced time. For urgent cases or questions, please email us with your payment screenshot.
4 Q: 已付款基礎攤位費，但想加購通行憑證？
A: 階段為唯一的加購申請與付款時段，付款完成後無法再更改選項，請務必於付款前確認。 		Q: I have paid the basic fee, but want to add an extra pass?
A: The current phase is the only period for add-on applications and payments. Once payment is completed, options cannot be changed. Please confirm before paying.
5 Q: 是否有開發票或收據？
A: 網站自動開立電子發票，請確認報名時所填Email。 		Q: Will I receive an invoice or receipt?
A: The website automatically issues an e-invoice. Please make sure your email is correct when registering.
6 Q: 我還可以加購通行憑證嗎？
A: 目前不開放加購。如需入場，可至攤商管理頁面購買「親友快速通關票」。 		Q: Can I purchase additional access passes?
A: Additional access passes are currently not available. If someone needs entry, please purchase a "Fast Track Friend Pass" through the Exhibitor Management Page.
7 Q: 親友快速通關票怎麼買？
A: 購票時間為 11/20–11/23。請從攤商管理頁面提供的連結購買（建議由攤主統一處理），並輸入優惠代碼。每個代碼限單次下單，建議先統計數量後一次購買。 		Q: How do I buy the Fast Track Friend Pass?
A: Sales are open from 11/20–11/23. Please use the purchase link provided in the Exhibitor Management Page (we recommend the exhibitor handle all purchases for their friends) and enter the discount code. Each code can be used for one order only, so please confirm the quantity needed before placing a single order.
8 Q: 通行憑證可以讓不同人使用嗎？
A: 可以。但活動入場以「紋身貼紙」為憑證，通行憑證僅作識別使用。 		Q: Can access passes be used by different people?
A: Yes. However, entry to the event requires the "tattoo sticker" as the admission credential. The access pass is for identification only.
9 Q: 為什麼攤商編號貼紙的字體顏色不一樣？
A: 黃色字體代表首次參與的攤商，歡迎大家多多交流！ 		Q: Why are the booth number sticker text colors different?
A: Yellow text indicates exhibitors who are joining us for the first time—please feel free to say hi!
10 Q: 我可以換位置嗎？
A: 可以！只要雙方協調同意即可自行交換攤位位置。但官方公布的攤位地圖將不會調整。 		Q: Can I change my booth location?
A: Yes! As long as both parties agree, you may swap booths directly. However, the official venue map will not be updated.
11 Q: 草率簿可以多拿幾本嗎？
A: 每攤限領一本。如需額外購買，可至現場「草率大本攤」。 		Q: Can I take more copies of the Catalog?
A: Each booth may claim one copy. Additional copies can be purchased at the "NMHW by TPABF" onsite.
12 Q: 材料區怎麼沒有多餘的佈置材料？
A: 現場僅提供少量可自取的佈置材料，數量有限、恕不補充，請酌量使用。 		Q: Why are there no extra materials in the materials area?
A: Only a limited amount of booth-decoration materials will be provided onsite. Please take only what you need—quantities are limited and will not be replenished.
13 Q: 我想在 NMHW 寄賣書籍，可以嗎？
A: 11/23 16:00 後，我們僅接受先前有填寫表單的寄售攤商交付未售完書籍，不開放臨時加收品項喔。 		Q: I'd like to consign books at NMHW as well. Is that possible?
A: After 16:00 on 11/23, we will only accept unsold books from exhibitors who previously submitted the consignment form. We are unable to accept any additional or last-minute items.
14 Q: 我也想參明年草率季十週年！！
A: 即日起至 11 月 23 日 點此表單 勾選「＊2026十週年報名我要參加＊」將直接獲取錄取資格。並維持與2025年相同之攤位費用。 		Q: I want to join the 10th Anniversary edition of TPABF next year!
A: From now until November 23rd, you may fill out this form and check "＊I want to join the 2026 10th Anniversary＊" to secure your spot. The booth fee will be the same as in 2025.
