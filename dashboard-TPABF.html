FAQ

(綠取階段) (Application Phase)
1 Q: 我該不該加購通行憑證呢？
A: 我們提供了一個簡易計算表格，只需填寫攤位類型、桌數以及預計進場人數，系統將會提供最佳加購建議，幫助您避免錯誤購買。歡迎善加利用！ 		Q: Should I purchase additional access passes?
A: We have provided a simple calculation spreadsheet. By entering your booth type, number of tables, and expected number of attendees, the system will suggest the optimal number of passes to purchase, helping you avoid incorrect purchases. We encourage you to make use of it!
2 Q: 同一天的通行憑證可以讓不同人使用嗎？
A: 不可以。通行憑證為刺青貼紙形式，每張貼紙於同一天僅限一人使用，當日不可更換使用者，亦不可跨日使用。 		Q: Can the same-day access pass be used by different people?
A: No. Access passes are provided as temporary tattoo stickers. Each sticker may only be used by one person per day and cannot be transferred to another person on the same day or used across different days.
3 Q: 如果我需要更多通行憑證，可以加購嗎？
A: 每個攤位最多僅能加購 1 份通行憑證。如有超過需求，請依實際活動天數與人數，另行購買入場票券。

（親友預售票將於 01/26–02/08 開賣。）
（各票種與相關資訊，請持續關注我們的 IG 貼文喔～～） 		Q: Can I purchase more access passes if needed?
A: Each booth may purchase up to one additional access pass. If you need more, please purchase admission tickets based on the actual number of event days and attendees.

(Friends & Family Ticket available from 01/26 to 02/08.)
(For ticket types and updates, please follow our Instagram for more information.)
4 Q:可以用什麼繳費？
A:繳費頁面有說明支援的付款方式，信用卡、apple pay、銀行轉帳（轉帳已關閉）。 		Q: What payment methods are available?
A: Supported payment methods are listed on the payment page, including credit card, Apple Pay, and bank transfer(bank transfer is now closed).
5 Q: 如何確認是否付款成功？
A: 完成付款後，訂單的付款狀態將會在官方商店中顯示。 		Q: How can I confirm if my payment was successful?
A: After completing payment, the payment status will be shown in your order on the official store.
6 Q: 我已完成付款，但參展資格狀態尚未更新？
A: 請耐心等候，團隊將於 1 月 5 日前 統一更新「攤商管理頁面」的狀態。如有急件或任何疑問，請附上付款截圖來信洽詢。 		Q: I’ve completed the payment, but my Exhibitor Status hasn’t been updated.
A: Please allow some time for processing. The team will update the status on the Exhibitor Management Page by January 5. If you have any urgent matters or questions, please contact us and include a screenshot of your payment.
7 Q: 我已完成付款，還可以更改加購的桌數或通行憑證數量嗎？
A: 本階段為唯一的加購申請時段（至 12/31 為止）。完成繳費後恕不接受任何變更，請於付款前確認所需的加購項目。 		Q: I’ve completed the payment. Can I still change the number of additional tables or access passes?
A: This is the only period for add-on applications (until Dec 31). No changes will be accepted after payment is completed, so please confirm all add-on items before payment.
8 Q: 是否會提供發票或收據？
A: 台灣訂單將由網站自動開立電子發票，請確認報名時填寫的 Email 正確。海外單位如需開立發票，請填寫此表單；提交後，我們將盡快將發票寄送至您的電子郵件信箱。 		Q: Will an invoice or receipt be provided?
A: Overseas participants who require an invoice should complete this form; once submitted, the invoice will be sent to your email as soon as possible.
9 Q:攤位費可以開發票打統編嗎？
A:可以，繳費處可填寫發票抬頭。 		Q: Can I request an invoice with my company tax ID for the booth fee?
A: Yes. You may fill in the invoice details at the payment page.

A: :可以先參考以往攤位的展示方式，實際攤位配置方式可參考攤商管理頁面之攤主手冊（一月底寄出）。 		Q: Will there be a back wall for the booth? Is it possible to switch to a wall-side booth?

9 Q: 常見付款方式有哪些？
A: :大部分訪客偏好使用現金，但也有不少人使用信用卡或 Apple Pay。PayPal 較少見，但偶爾有人使用。如果可能，建議提供至少一種數位支付方式，但僅收現金也完全沒問題。 		Q: What are the common payment methods?
A: Most visitors prefer cash, but many also use credit cards and Apple Pay. PayPal is less common but still used occasionally. If possible, it’s a good idea to offer at least one digital payment option, though accepting cash only is also fine.
